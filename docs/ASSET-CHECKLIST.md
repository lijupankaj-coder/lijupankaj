## Upload checks

Before upload, confirm that each file:

- belongs to that exact project and year;
- is approved for public portfolio use;
- contains no internal comments, names, email addresses, budgets or client data;
- is not an editable AI, PSD, INDD or working presentation file;
- has a meaningful filename, caption and alt text;
- is not a low-resolution preview or unrelated stock/generated image;
- has an intentional cover crop and focal point at mobile and desktop sizes.

The approved internal applications remain text-based case studies until public-safe screenshots are confirmed. Do not invent interface mockups. If screenshots are later approved, crop or redact all employee, client, account and credential data before upload.
