About Me

Selected Work

Personal case studies shaped by real creative and operational needs.

Case study 01

Business Operations Platform

Problem or requirement
Growing businesses needed sales, quotations, invoices, inventory, procurement, HR, payroll, documents, approvals, and reporting in one connected workspace.
My role
I shaped the product architecture, mapped the business workflows, planned the interface, and directed the AI-assisted build and release verification.
Approach
I organized the system around company-scoped workspaces, connected modules, role-based access, audit trails, and clear operational handoffs.
Technologies or methods
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind CSS, document generation, workflow mapping, and AI-assisted product development.
Final result
A live multi-tenant cloud ERP with isolated company workspaces, connected business modules, server-side authorization, and protected private routes.

Case study 02

Workforce Notification System

Problem or requirement
Managers needed timely access to their assigned Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central notifications on desktop and mobile, including when the application was closed.
My role
I translated the operational requirement into a focused product experience, planned the system flow, and directed the AI-assisted implementation and verification.
Approach
I kept approvals inside Business Central while providing scoped notifications, protected administration, responsive navigation, PWA installation, and closed-app alerts.
Technologies or methods
Next.js, React, Supabase Auth/PostgreSQL/Realtime, Microsoft Entra OAuth, standards-based Web Push, PWA planning, and role-based UX.
Final result
A deployed internal application with assigned-user notifications, in-app popups, closed-app Web Push alerts, and a mobile-friendly installed experience.

Case study 03

Creative Agency Workflow

Problem or requirement
Agency operations were spread across briefs, campaigns, tasks, estimates, proposals, job orders, production, accounts, and job-closing activities.
My role
I mapped the agency workflow, planned the information architecture and interfaces, and shaped the platform through AI-assisted product development.
Approach
I connected the full job lifecycle in a structured pipeline, with organization-scoped access and clear transitions between creative, commercial, production, and finance work.
Technologies or methods
TanStack Start, React, Supabase, workflow architecture, organization-scoped permissions, interface systems, and iterative product planning.
Final result
A connected agency operations platform that brings campaign delivery, estimating, proposals, production, accounts, and closing into one coherent workflow.

Case study 04

Creative Production Utilities

Problem or requirement
Design and production teams repeatedly handle focused tasks such as mockup creation, brand-kit preparation, compression, background removal, estimation, email building, vector conversion, and QR generation.
My role
I identified practical use cases, planned each focused experience, created the visual direction, and guided development with modern AI-assisted tools.
Approach
I treated each task as a small, clear workflow with minimal inputs, immediate feedback, and an output suited to everyday creative production.
Technologies or methods
Static HTML and JavaScript, React and Next.js where appropriate, client-side processing, WASM/WebGL, image-generation services, and responsive interface design.
Final result
A working collection of focused utilities covering mockup creation, brand kits, compression, background removal, estimation, email building, vector conversion, and QR generation.
